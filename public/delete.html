Account Deletion

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Al-Muzayyin account and related personal data from the mobile app and related services.

1. How to request account deletion

You can request deletion in either of these ways:

  1. Open the Al-Muzayyin app → Settings → Help center (or contact support) and send a message asking to delete your account.
  2. Email us from the same email address linked to your account with the subject line “Account deletion request” via the website contact channel at https://elmzin.caesar-agency.co.uk, or through in-app support.

Please include:

  • Full name on the account
  • Registered phone number and/or email
  • A clear statement that you want the account deleted
We may ask you to verify ownership of the account before completing the deletion request.

2. What happens after you request deletion

  • We review and process valid requests within a reasonable period, typically within 30 days.
  • After deletion, you will no longer be able to sign in with that account.
  • Profile information associated with the account will be removed from active systems.

3. Data that is deleted

When an account deletion is completed, we delete or anonymize:

  • Account profile details (name, email, phone, photo)
  • App preferences (language, appearance, notification settings)
  • Saved address linked to the account, where applicable
  • Favorites and similar personal app preferences
  • Access credentials so the account can no longer be used to sign in

4. Data that may be retained for a limited time

Some records may be kept for a limited period when required for legitimate business, security, fraud prevention, accounting, or legal obligations. Examples may include:

  • Completed booking or shop-order records needed for salon operations, dispute handling, or financial records
  • Security and audit logs
  • Information a salon already received for a past booking (name/phone shared at booking time), which may remain in that salon’s own records

Where retention is required, we keep only what is necessary and for no longer than needed.

5. Salon / business accounts

If your account is linked to a salon or beauty center profile, deleting the user account may also remove or deactivate related salon management access, team settings, and media owned by that account, subject to any open bookings or legal retention needs.

6. Related policy

For more detail about how we handle personal information, see our Privacy Policy.

7. Contact

Questions about account deletion can be sent through Al-Muzayyin app support or https://elmzin.caesar-agency.co.uk.
