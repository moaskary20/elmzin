Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Al-Muzayyin (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Al-Muzayyin mobile application and related online services (the “Service”).

1. Who we are

Al-Muzayyin is a booking and marketplace platform that connects customers with beauty centers and salons, and also offers product shopping features. The Service is operated in connection with elmzin.caesar-agency.co.uk.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the Service, we may collect:

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number, profile photo, preferred language, appearance settings, and notification preferences.
  • Location and address: map-selected address, city/district, and approximate coordinates used to show nearby salons, home-service options, and delivery/booking context.
  • Booking information: selected salon, service, specialist, date/time, customer name, and phone number needed to confirm the appointment.
  • Shop orders: product selections, quantities, delivery address, payment method choice, and (if card payment is selected) limited card reference details such as last four digits. We do not store full card numbers in the app.
  • Salon operator content: for salon accounts, business profile data, working hours, services, team members, media (photos/videos), and verification documents such as health certificates when provided.
  • Device and usage data: basic technical information needed to operate the app (for example app version, request logs, and crash/diagnostic signals where available).

3. How we use information

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Show salons, services, availability, and product catalog.
  • Process bookings and shop orders.
  • Share booking contact details with the selected salon.
  • Provide customer support and improve the Service.
  • Send service-related notifications you enable.
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations.

4. How information is shared

  • With salons: when you book, your name and phone number (and relevant booking details) are shared with that salon so they can confirm and serve the appointment.
  • With service providers: hosting, maps, and infrastructure partners that help us run the Service, under appropriate safeguards.
  • For legal reasons: if required by law, regulation, or to protect rights, safety, and security.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Permissions used by the app

  • Location / maps: to choose an address and find nearby salons.
  • Photos / media: to set a profile photo or upload salon media when you choose to do so.
  • Internet access: to sync account data, bookings, and catalog content with our servers.

6. Data retention

We keep account, booking, and order records for as long as needed to provide the Service and for legitimate business, accounting, and legal purposes. You may request deletion of your account data by contacting us.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children’s privacy

The Service may help customers book kids-related salon services, but it is intended for use by adults (parents/guardians) managing those bookings. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children without parental involvement.

9. Your choices

  • Update profile details and preferences in the app settings.
  • Control notification preferences in the app.
  • Request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us.

10. International processing

Information may be processed on servers operated for Al-Muzayyin and its hosting providers. By using the Service, you understand that your information may be transferred and stored in locations where we or our providers operate.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we publish revisions. Continued use of the Service after an update means you accept the revised policy.

12. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us through the Al-Muzayyin app support channels or via the website at https://elmzin.caesar-agency.co.uk.
